- CLEAN (the default): fillers ("uh", "um", "ah", "er", "hmm"), dead air and clear slips go. The rules already cut
  stutters, false starts and repeated words / phrases (never a repeat across a sentence end);
- a clear slip or self-correction the rules cannot see -> remove the misspoken WORDS only when what is left is a
  grammatical sentence that says what the speaker meant, using his own words ("Proverbs 30, 29 verse 18" -> remove
  "30,"; "Vision is a statement of what we are supposed to be doing. I mean, mission. While vision is a picture" ->
  remove the misspoken sentence, "I mean, mission." and "While"). Never invent, reorder or paraphrase words;
- a repeat that is intentional emphasis ("very, very", "no, no, no"), a rhetorical restart or a clarification that
  adds meaning -> keep it; when unsure, keep;
