AI Video Editor credits
===============

Herk Edit Kit, by Nate Herk (MIT)
  https://github.com/nateherkai/herk-edit-kit
  AI Video Editor is built on Herk's kit: its editing playbook and house style, the caption preset (from Herk's
  porter-clipper-short project), the style library and templates in style-library/ and style-templates/,
  and the HyperFrames render workflow. All credit for that work goes to Nate Herk.

Everything else in this repository is AI Video Editor's own work (MIT, see LICENSE). AI Video Editor's icons
(ai-video-editor/engine/icons.mjs) and sound effects (ai-video-editor/audio/sfx-synth.mjs) are original and generated locally.

Third-party software that install.sh or npm installs on your machine (none of it is bundled in this repo) is
listed with its licence in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

Music is never bundled. When a mode uses music, a CC0 or CC BY track is fetched from Openverse at render time
and the CREDITS.txt written next to that export names the track, its author, its licence and its source. Ship
that file with your video.

Sample clip used in README.md (downloaded by you, not bundled): "The Numbers Are In: Where Does 2021 Rank for
Global Temperatures" interview, NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, public domain.
